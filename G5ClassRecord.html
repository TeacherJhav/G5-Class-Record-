📝 Teacher Manual & Features

Welcome to your premium Grade 5 Class Record System. Here are the features at your disposal:

  • Preloaded Classes: Classes 501 to 506 are preloaded with their specific schedules, timers, and official student groups (including empty slots ready to be filled out).
  • Customizable Category Weights: Tweak weights inside category headers. Make sure total weight sums up to exactly 100%!
  • Dynamic Multi-Week Attendance: Switch between the "Grades" and "Weekly Attendance" sheets. Log status (Present, Absent, Late, Excused) by clicking each student's weekly badge. This does not alter the grade average calculations.
  • Row Selection: Click a student's row or name to highlight the row. This provides a clear, highlighted guide to help you scan grade information accurately!
  • Grading Scale:
    • 97-100: Excellent 🌟 (Confetti Celebration!)
    • 90-96: Very Good ✨
    • 80-89: Good 👍
    • 75-79: Fair 👌
    • Below 75: Needs Work 💪
